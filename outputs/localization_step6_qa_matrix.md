# Localization Step 6 QA Matrix

All `P1`–`P14` formal rows execute isolated English and Simplified Chinese tracks. The English track asserts Effective=`en` with no app translator. The Simplified Chinese track asserts Preferred=`zh_CN`, Effective=`zh_CN`, and a real app translator loaded from a freshly compiled official QM. Each track has its own app root, settings, database, backup/log state, and application/session state.
